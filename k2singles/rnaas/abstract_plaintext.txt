LaCourse & Jacobs (2018) catalogued 164 K2 events seen only once. I report periods for 15 of them from repeats in later K2 campaigns and TESS, and test them with a blind search of all 163 stars, with thresholds from the same search on inverted light curves. The blind search finds 11 of the 15 periods among its allowed periods; it misses both planet candidates. A search of the literature, VizieR catalogues and SPOC validation products finds no prior orbital period for six. TOI-5560.01 narrows from at most 702 d to 140.494 or 70.247 d, excluding four SPOC threshold-crossing events. The K2 candidate EPIC 211503363 has P = 122.905 or 61.453 d, but its companion is about 2.1 Jupiter radii, probably an M dwarf. The primary eclipse of EPIC 202126877 deepened from 6% in 2014 to 36% in 2026.
