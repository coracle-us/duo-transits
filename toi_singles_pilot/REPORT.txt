    Blind score on the 16 community-solved TOIs: 6 exact, 7 true period in our alias list, 3 not recovered
    (1 'allowed, not recovered', 2 'missed'; one of the 2 misses is most likely a wrong reference period).
  Pilot: 0 new claimable periods from 20 TOIs.

  Go/no-go for the remaining 68: NO-GO as specified (same ranking). See section 5 for the one variant worth
  running (32 high-SNR TOIs without any community period, expected yield a handful at best).

1. METHOD  (scripts/common.py, s01_run.py)
  Reused, validated code: k2singles/scripts/k2lib.py v3 (grid / find_repeats / robust_periods), alias_pipeline
  load_lightcurves + test_window, pilot.compute_diffs / plot_windows, diff_image.analyse, eclipse_times.fit_event.
  - Light curves: one product per sector (SPOC 120 s > TESS-SPOC > QLP > SPOC 20 s), quality == 0. Sectors that
    TESScut lists without any product get our own aperture photometry (tesscut_photometry algorithm, written
    into toi_singles_pilot/data). Own photometry filled 21 target-sectors.
    - Pilot: 1301.02 S59; 2098.01 S15, S85; 2102.02 S56; 2263.01 S60; 2270.01 S20, S26; 2298.01 S73;
      2433.01 S47; 4585.01 S48, S74; 6075.01 S59, S79; 6690.02 S94, S96; 6691.01 S2, S29, S35.
    - Validation: 1894.01 S29; 4187.02 S106; 6663.01 S53.
    Other TESScut sectors gave 0 good cadences (off-chip or flagged). For 4348.01 the TESScut search failed.
  - Transits of sibling TOIs with a real period (not single/duo/max-period) are masked.
  - Box test of the TOI depth/duration every D/4; repeats need SNR >= 8, depth 0.5-2x, coverage >= 0.8, baseline
    on both sides; periods from robust_periods (P >= 5 d, every repeat can anchor, flat windows exclude).
  - Allowed periods on a fine period grid (step such that the furthest epoch moves <= D/8; fixes review point
    M3): min allowed P, fraction of log P excluded, allowed ranges (in each JSON).
  - Change made BEFORE freezing the blind answers (prompted by our own event check, not by any reference):
    if the TOI epoch is not itself a 'supported' dip (TOI epochs can be propagated reference epochs), the
    highest-SNR robust dip becomes the anchor. Affected 1895.01, 6890.01 (validation), 2296.01 (pilot).
  - Added AFTER the validation comparison (so not blind): s08_screen_repeats.py, a trapezoid shape screen on
    every repeat (fit converges; depth 0.5-2x and T14 0.67-1.5x the event's fit; data on both sides; no gap in
    transit), periods recomputed from passing repeats only.
    Post-hoc on validation: it removed 5 spurious repeats (gap-edge ramps, half-depth dips) and no true one.
    1894.01 tightens from 6 to 2 periods ({369.62, 739.24}, truth 369.60).
    2300.03 loses its wrong 914 d answer.
    All 6 exact answers survive.
  - Vetting tools: s05_vet_events.py (trapezoid fits in every product: SPOC 120/20 s, TESS-SPOC, QLP;
    ephemeris), s09_diff_at.py (difference image at a given time), s06_gaia_flags.py (RUWE, NSS orbit, RV
    variability), s04_novelty_check.py (fresh ExoFOP JSON + live pscomppars).

  Blindness caveat: toi_singles_k2/REPORT.txt, which I was told to read first, quotes 6 of the 16 reference
  periods as examples (772.02, 1894.01, 4629.01, 5990.01, 2300.03, 4187.02). No pipeline parameter was tuned. The
  only pre-freeze change (re-anchoring) was triggered by two events not being recovered at the TOI epoch.
  The frozen file has a timestamp (validation_blind.csv, frozen_at 2026-10-12).

  produce confident wrong periods.

4. WHAT CHANGES FOR ANY FURTHER RUN
  - Check ALL community period uploads, not only those dated after the TOI update. Of the remaining 68 TOIs,
    12 already have a community period upload of some date (remaining_pool.csv, pre_upload).
  - Rank by per-transit SNR, not by new-sector count.
  - Keep the shape screen (s08) and the multi-product refit (s05) as standard steps.
  - Add a SAP fallback for quality-flagged cadences and an edge/partial-event test. These cost 2 of the 16
    validation recoveries.
  - Difference-image offsets with snr_peak < ~5, or near a saturated neighbour, are unreliable; read the
    diff/err map instead.

5. GO / NO-GO
  - Remaining 68 ranked as before: NO-GO. The pilot took its best-covered 20 and produced 0 claimable periods.
    - 17/20 were SNR~7-8 TOIs where the method is blind.
    - The 2 that worked were already solved by the community in 2022.
    - The rest of the pool has fewer new sectors (median 2, max 7).
  - Only variant worth doing: the 32 remaining TOIs with TOI SNR >= 15 and no community period upload of any
    date (remaining_pool.csv).
    - Run it without diff images except on screened hits; ~2-3 h wall, a few GB.
    - Expected yield, rough: the remaining TOIs have ~2.5 new sectors each, which gives roughly a 20-30% chance
      per TOI of a transit landing in new data, and part of the pool is EB/SB2-prone (e.g. 224.01).
    - That is ~3-6 TOIs with a new transit: mostly duo -> alias-list narrowings, 0-3 full resolutions.
    - slee and mason upload every few weeks, so some will be scooped.
    - That is a short RNAAS table at best. If the goal is a publishable novelty yield, stop here.
