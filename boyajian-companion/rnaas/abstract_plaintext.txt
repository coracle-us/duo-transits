A symmetric, 1.1%-deep, ~21 h single transit of KIC 8462852 in TESS Sector 15 has been attributed to a ~9 M_J companion with five Kepler-allowed period windows between 1030 and 1368 d, while a recurrence model of the star's dimming families favors ~780 d. I test trial periods from 770 to 845 d, and the five windows, against Kepler long-cadence photometry under one constraint: flux deficits add, so a transit cannot hide in data dimmed by less than the transit itself. Near 780 d, Kepler allows only 776.17-776.60, 790.50-790.66, 792.08-792.92 and 793.25-793.40 d, excluding the model's maximum-likelihood (781.6 d) and median (800.9 d) periods. The same test excludes window 4 (1345.65 d), whose earlier transit falls on a dip only 0.44% deep. Public AAVSO and ASAS-SN photometry can neither detect nor exclude a transit in window 3 (2026 October 18-23), the published favorite.
