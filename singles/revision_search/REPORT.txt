1. WHAT THE NOTE'S MATCHED SEARCH IS FOR, AND WHAT IT MISSES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The repeat search asks one question: does the CATALOGUED event recur? Only a recurrence of the
same event (same object, same eclipse) can be used in P = dT/n; the aliases and the flat-data
exclusions are all computed for that one event. Fixing depth (0.5-2x) and duration to the
catalogued values is what makes a detection a repeat of THAT event and keeps the number of
trials (and false repeats) low. A dip of a different depth is not a repeat: a secondary eclipse
sits at an unknown phase (eccentric orbits), and a different body has its own period, so
neither can enter P = dT/n. By construction the matched search therefore misses (i) EB
eclipses of a different depth (secondaries, or a deeper primary when the catalogued event was
the secondary), (ii) transits of other bodies, (iii) repeats of a different duration, and
(iv) repeats where the CTOI depth itself is wrong. The search below covers those.

2. METHOD (rules fixed in the search_any.py / classify.py docstrings before results)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Same light curves as the note: SPOC 2-min, TESS-SPOC, QLP and our TESScut photometry (OWN,
S>=100 without products); best product per sector for the search; every detection re-measured
in every other product. Free depth; durations 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 24 h plus the catalogued D.
Detection: SNR >= 8 including a red-noise factor (measurable), coverage >= 0.8, baseline on
both sides in the same segment (>= max(0.25 d, D/2)), >= 0.25 d from segment/sector/orbit
edges, momentum-dump test, median depth >= half the mean depth, depth >= 3x the 1-h scatter.
Control: the identical search on 2 - flux. Automatic (d) filter: seen on >= 2 other stars
within 0.5 d (common-mode), not confirmed in another reduction (SNR >= 4, depth 0.5-2x), no
other reduction available, or a 2-reduction control brightening on the same star at >= the SNR.
Changes after first results (disclosed): (1) dump rule v1 -> v2 (v1 vetoed 37 of 272 genuine
catalogued events, up to SNR 76; v2 vetoes only dumps in the event core whose two sides do not
both show the dip); (2) the depth/duration used to classify an event comes from a fine
duration refit (0.5-30 h), and the catalogued-event reference from the free fit at the
catalogued time and duration, because the coarse 6-step grid split single eclipse families;
(3) (b) uses durations within 1/3-3x. Detection thresholds were never changed.
Extra events with a dump inside the event window (would fail the v1 rule): 49 of 467 in the 272; 6 of 92 vetted-real.
