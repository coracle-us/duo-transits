Salinas et al. (2026) listed 108 single-transit candidates and 4 multi-planet systems from TESS Sectors 27-60. I searched Sectors 61-107 for repeats of each catalogued event. On 112 stars, 53 candidates show at least one matching event and 8 have three or more events on one period. Two are already TOIs, two are unreliable, and four are new. Their periods are 17.06402, 21.19694, 49.70152 and 23.75297 d. All four pass odd/even, difference-image and Gaia checks, and a blind re-analysis with separate code recovers each period within 1.6 sigma. TIC 241550976 and 107113048 look like warm giants on circular orbits. The transit of TIC 275647095 is too long for a circular orbit, and a marginal dip at phase 0.87 may make it an eclipsing binary. TIC 230133616 was never a single: it transits 37 times in the northern continuous viewing zone, and its host is a subgiant with an unresolved companion.
