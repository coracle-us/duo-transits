B. REQUIRED FIXES (priority order)
----------------------------------
1. Results, first paragraph: "In none of them does any available pipeline light curve (SPOC SAP and
   PDCSAP, TESS-SPOC, QLP, or five other reductions) show an event that my reductions miss."
   Problems: (i) it never says which light curve the event test ran on. The decisive fact for the
   editor's Q6 is in REPORT.txt but not in the note: the event test ALWAYS ran on pipeline
   products at the catalogued epoch (SPOC 2-min 285 stars, TESS-SPOC 7, QLP 8, no data 6; own
   photometry never, since every catalogued epoch precedes S100). (ii) As written it is false:
   TIC 38005437 passes in SAP (3.3 ppt, SNR 7.2), TESS-SPOC (SNR 6.0) and own photometry while
   PDCSAP (the product used) gives SNR 3.3; TIC 355095316 passes in QLP KSPSAP (SNR 8.5) vs PDCSAP
   4.1; TIC 425585592 passes in TESS-SPOC (SNR 5.8) vs SPOC 2-min 3.9; TIC 66013259 is flagged in
   SPOC/QLP but TARS shows it at SNR 6.7. (iii) "five other reductions" is six: TGLC, eleanor,
   T16, CDIPS, TARS, TASOC (audit_34.csv).
   Suggested text: "The event test used pipeline light curves at every catalogued epoch (SPOC
   2-min for 285 stars), never my own photometry. I inspected the 31 failures in every available
   light curve (SPOC SAP and PDCSAP, TESS-SPOC, QLP, six other reductions, and my own photometry).
   None fails because of my photometry; in three near-threshold cases (TIC 38005437, 355095316,
   425585592) a different pipeline product would pass the test."
   Same fix in the letter, item 6 ("No pipeline light curve shows an event that my reductions
   miss" and "rather than differences in photometry" overstate; 38005437 is a PDCSAP-specific
   suppression, i.e. a detrending difference).

2. Results, any-depth paragraph: "Almost all extra eclipses fall on stars already classed as
   binaries: they give new periods for four of them, and for two the catalogued event is the
   secondary of a deeper eclipse."
   Evidence (extra_events.csv, vetted real): 92 events on 14 stars; by class EB-on-target 52
   events/7 stars, off-target EB 5/2, repeats-inconsistent 34/4, single 1/1. So 34 of 92 events
   and 4 of 14 stars are in the "no single period" class, not "classed as binaries". Two of the
   four new periods (188835620, 22.217 d; 83384234, ~4.33 d) are for repeats-inconsistent stars,
   and 162031311 (also repeats-inconsistent) shows a 5 ppt eclipse twice the catalogued depth.
   This is exactly the editor's point in Q5 (the matched search hides EBs) and should be stated,
   not hidden. Suggested: "The extra eclipses fall on 14 stars: 9 already classed as binaries,
   4 of the 23 without a single period, and one single. They give periods to four (two of them
   from the no-single-period class: TIC 188835620, 22.217 d, and TIC 83384234, 4.33 d), and for
   two the catalogued event is the secondary of a deeper eclipse."

3. Results bullet "23 show repeats that no single period explains" is literally false: the
   candidate-period grid is 5-1500 d (pilot.P_GRID), and at least three of the 23 are short-period
   EBs (53847727, the known 3.01-d Prsa EB used as a positive control; 83384234, ~4.33 d;
   76804724, ~3.7 d spacing; period_recheck/README.md). Add "above 5 d": "23 show repeats that no
   single period of 5-1500 d explains". Also say in Methods that candidate periods are searched
   over 5-1500 d (currently only implied by the abstract's log-period range).

4. "Both eclipses are on target." (147035057 paragraph) is asserted without evidence for the
   deep eclipse: no difference image was made for S6 or S87 (no TESScut cutouts exist for this
   star). Either run scripts/diff_image.py on S6/S87 at the deep-eclipse ephemeris, or replace
   with the argument that holds: "The deep eclipse cannot come from a neighbour: the brightest
   Gaia stars within 40 arcsec (G = 13.5 at 17 and 24 arcsec) supply about 1% of the flux." The
   shallow eclipse's localisation is "mixed" in final_classification (S7 offset 6.3 +/- 1.1
   arcsec, S33/S108 2-3 arcsec), so "on target" for it should read "on target in S33 and S108".

5. "Only TIC 250492521 shows a convincing extra event" vs. the sentence two lines earlier that
   "extra dips below SNR ~20 are consistent with noise". The 250492521 event has SNR 10.0
   (extra_events.csv), inside the regime the note itself calls noise-like; 29 of the 92 vetted
   "real" events are below SNR 20 (23 of them on the 3.28-d EB 75577433). Either drop
   "convincing" ("one single star, TIC 250492521, shows a second event of different shape, seen
   in three reductions, that makes it an EB or blend candidate") or qualify the noise statement
   ("as a population, extra dips below SNR ~20 are no more common than inverted-light-curve
   artefacts; individual ones were accepted only with multi-reduction confirmation").

6. Sample: "Of 794 open CTOIs flagged as single or duo transits" -> the selection is "with no
   period, or whose notes or tag mention single, duo or mono" (build_ctoi_singles.py). Since this
   sentence is the direct answer to Q3, it must describe the actual rule. Also verify that
   ExoFOP user "skinner" (17 CTOIs) is a TSTPC member; I could not check offline. The letter
   makes the same "flagged as single or duo" claim.

7. Word limit risk. RNAAS states "1,500 words or fewer" without defining the count. Abstract +
   body is ~1436 (my count) / 1437 (author's), but with the AI disclosure (68) and the enlarged
   acknowledgments (55) the manuscript text is ~1560. The submitted version was ~960 + extras.
   Either confirm with the AAS submission form's counter or cut ~70 words (candidates: the
   HD 88986 b example sentence, 28 words; "On known planets the offsets are 1-3''", 8; the
   duplicated "1--3" sentence about partial-transit sweep, 25).

C. SHOULD FIX (accuracy, cheap)
-------------------------------
8. Methods photometry: "11 stars (T=8.5-15.5)" but "backgrounds agree to 1-9%" and "median 1.07"
   exclude the T=15.5 star (29%; median over 11 is 1.10). Say "11 stars (T = 8.5-15.5), ten of
   them usable (the T = 15.5 star is 96% contaminated)" or quote 10 stars, T = 8.5-14.1.
9. "up to 30% shallower": the worst case is 0.69, i.e. 31%. Write "about 30%" or "up to a third".
10. Table comment: "classification of all 303 stars is available in the repository" but the
    frozen CSV has 306 rows (34 event-not-recovered). Say "all 306 CTOIs (303 stars)".
11. "The search re-detects 234 of the 272 catalogued events": say why 38 fail (21 are below the
    SNR 8 cut, 6 straddle momentum dumps) so the reader does not read it as a 14% miss rate of
    real eclipses. Also 4 failures are unexplained ('ok' in recovery_check.csv: free fit passes
    at SNR 8.8-11.1 but the search returned nothing) -> worth one look.
12. Letter item 5: "finds the secondary eclipses of all six known TESS EBs" -> "six of six, one
    of them (TIC 396720998) seen in QLP only and as deep as the primary, so the catalogued
    period may be twice too short". Also disclose that the momentum-dump rule was revised after
    first results (v1 vetoed 37 genuine catalogued events); REPORT.txt discloses it, the note and
    letter do not.
13. Letter "Other changes" omits unrequested edits a moderator will notice: the new Sample
    sentences (22 stars outside the cuts, the pilot target in the TESS EB catalogue), the removal
    of the grazing-fit numbers (dBIC, k, R2, RUWE) from the 147035057 paragraph, and the new
    title. List them.
14. 147035057 depth: the note gives 5.6% three times. verify_147035057.py gives 5.3% minimum
    depth in both S6 and S87; the box search gives 4.1%; the vetting note 4.1-5.7%. "about 5%" is
    safer, or state it is the V-shaped minimum from S87 (QLP, 200 s).
15. "17 were vetted by hand" etc. fine, but "repeats-inconsistent" star 53847727 is the pilot
    target that is a known Prsa EB; the Sample says it was "later found in the TESS EB
    catalogue" without naming it. Name it (TIC 53847727) so the table/CSV match.
