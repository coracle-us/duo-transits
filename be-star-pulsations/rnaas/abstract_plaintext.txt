Be stars build and lose their decretion disks over months to years. I compare TESS light curves with the NEOWISE W1 light curves of 1,084 Be-star candidates from the catalogue of Jia et al. (2026), sector by sector and within each star. When the W1 disk excess is larger, the star is more variable in the optical. The variability amplitude below 1 c/d rises by about 8% per 0.1 mag of excess and passes a null test that swaps disk histories between stars (p < 1e-4). It also rises in the 256 stars with H-alpha emission in their Gaia spectra. At higher frequencies the rise is about 3%, and the dominant periodic signal shows no separate response. Stars whose excess changed most carry the trend: of 177 that changed by more than 0.4 mag, 71% show it. This is a population tendency, not a rule for each star.
