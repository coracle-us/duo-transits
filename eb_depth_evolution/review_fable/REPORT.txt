LOGIC OF THE KEY CLAIMS (verdicts)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "dilution cannot alter S/P": correct. Any third light in a given season multiplies both depths by the same
  factor 1/(1+l3); the ratio within a season is invariant even if l3 differs between seasons. Wording "no
  constant dilution" is fine (and could be stronger: no dilution at all).
- "opposite O-C of P and S means e cos w changes, not light-travel time": correct. LTTE and a period change
  move both eclipses together; only a change in e cos w (or e, w) moves them apart. Verified numerically
  (+13.3 / -12.9 min in 2021). Secular apsidal motion would also do this, which is why the GR/tidal comparison
  is needed; it holds (above).
- "tidal/GR apsidal motion orders of magnitude too slow": correct, GR 0.0035 deg/yr, classical < 0.01 deg/yr
  at R/a 0.03-0.04 even with fast rotation, vs observed >~ 0.4 deg/yr secular and ~5-10 deg/yr within seasons.
- "a turnaround before 2014 would predict 4-7% in 2004-08": true only for the quadratic (R7).
- "outer period ~100-600 d": the formula is the standard nodal-precession period (Soderhjelm 1975); the range
  follows from the stated assumptions, which the text should name (S7). i_mut >= ~2.5 deg is needed for the
  >= 4.8 deg swing from onset to totality; 2 deg is slightly too small.
- "Gaia RUWE mildly favours a low-mass tertiary": fair, with the P2 caveat (S8).
- "rate rose from 0.20 to 0.31; constant excluded": the rise and the exclusion are robust (a constant TESS-era
  rate underpredicts the 2014 depth by 6x); the number 0.31 is not ours (R1).
