OPEN SINGLE / DUO TOIs x K2 (+ new TESS data): SCOPING AND LIGHT PILOT
2026-10-12. Inputs: data/raw/exofop_toi.csv (2026-10-11), data/raw/tess_orbit_times.csv, MAST, VizieR,
ExoFOP target JSON (fetched 2026-10-12). Nothing in data/raw was edited; nothing committed.

VERDICT
  K2 route: NO-GO. Only 3 of the 156 TOIs sit on stars that K2 observed, and all 3 are explained:
    one is our own TOI-5560.01 (the control), one has its K2 events already assigned on ExoFOP,
    and one shows nothing in K2.
  New-TESS route: possible, but crowded. 88 TOIs have TESS sectors observed after their last TOI
    update and no newer period on ExoFOP. However, community users (slee, mason, osborn, vincent,
    lhill) posted 2025-26 periods for 15 others in the same list, so they are working this exact
    seam. Go ahead only as a small pilot with a fresh ExoFOP check just before any claim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TARGET LIST  (scripts/s01_targets.py -> targets.csv)
  Rule: TFOPWG disposition not CP/KP/FP/FA, AND (period blank/0, OR comment matches
        single|duo|mono, OR comment matches a maximum-period phrase).
  Result: 156 TOIs on 154 stars (reproduces the 156). The categories overlap:
    77 have no period, 123 match single/duo/mono, 47 have a max-period comment.
  Refinements (added as flags; no rows dropped):
    - max_period_d parsed for 47 rows.
    - n_named_transits and last_sector_in_comment parsed.
    - comment_says_resolved = 1: TOI-7044.01, where the comment itself gives P = 15.97 d.
    - Ecliptic coordinates added.
  targets_merged.csv joins the ExoFOP and new-TESS columns (scripts/s08_summary.py).
  18 TOIs are on hosts that already have a confirmed planet in pscomppars.

2. K2 OVERLAP  (scripts/s02_k2_overlap.py -> k2_overlap.csv, derived/epic_matches.csv)
  Method: 49 TOIs lie within 25 deg of the ecliptic. For each, an EPIC cone search (VizieR IV/34,
  3") and a MAST query_region (3") were run. The MAST query looked for K2 target light curves,
  HLSPs, and K2 FFI footprints.
  - On a K2-observed star: 3 TOIs.
      TOI-5523.01 = EPIC 201205469 = K2-43, a confirmed 2-planet host. C1 only
                    (BTJD -189.6 to -109.7). No period.
      TOI-5560.01 = EPIC 211924561. C5, C16, C18. P_max 702.47 d.
                    All 140 aliases (P >= 5 d) put a transit in K2 data, because C16 sits at
                    exactly 2 x P_max before S46.
      TOI-5696.03 = EPIC 248618202. C14 (BTJD 905.8 to 985.4). No period.
                    This is a multi-planet system: .02 = TOI-5696 b (32.43 d, validated by
                    Collier et al. 2026, arXiv:2608.21560). .01 is a K2 + TESS duo
                    (P_max 1637.27 d) from Planet Hunters TESS.
  - In EPIC but never observed by K2: 2 TOIs (6929.01 = EPIC 201417046; 7869.01 = EPIC 245907996).
  - No target falls in a K2 FFI footprint without also being a K2 target.
  - "With K2 epochs covered":
      - 1 TOI (5560.01) by aliases.
      - 2 TOIs (5523.01, 5696.03) by a blind search over about 80 d of K2 data each.

3. K2 EPOCH TEST  (scripts/s03_k2_search.py, s09_annotate_hits.py -> k2_hits.csv, plots/)
  Search: k2lib v3 grid box test with the TOI depth and duration, then find_repeats.
  Four reductions:
    - K2 PDCSAP;
    - raw SAP;
    - EVEREST v2, with Kepler bits masked only. EVEREST's own outlier bit 2^24 flags real
      transits, e.g. TOI-5560's C5 and C16 cadences;
    - K2SFF.
  Each candidate is re-tested in every reduction. Masked and thruster cadences within +/-D are counted.

  Control, TOI-5560.01: both known K2 transits recovered.
    C5  BTJD 179.89: PDC 1860 ppm (SNR 22), EVEREST 1671 ppm (SNR 39), K2SFF 1761 ppm (SNR 37).
        SAP is ambiguous because of the roll sawtooth.
    C16 BTJD 1163.34: PDC 1717 ppm (SNR 25), SAP 1704 ppm (SNR 21), EVEREST 1694 ppm (SNR 19).
        K2SFF has a gap there.
    The K2-only period test leaves 140.494 d and 70.247 d allowed (derived/k2_period_limits.csv).

  Real K2 events found for TOI-5696.03 (box D = 8.09 h): all are catalogued signals, none new.
    - BTJD 925.89, seen in all 4 reductions at 1035-1150 ppm.
      This is TOI-5696.01's K2 transit, which ExoFOP already uses (TOI epoch 925.865).
    - BTJD 948.48 (all 4 reductions) and 980.85 (EVEREST only at threshold).
      Both match TOI-5696.02 = TOI-5696 b (predicted 948.453 and 980.883).
    - Pairing check (scripts/s06_toi5696_durations.py; trapezoid supersampled for K2 long cadence;
      plots/toi5696_events.png). The K2 event matches .01, not .03, so the ExoFOP pairing stands:
        K2 C14 event:   T14 5.7-6.8 h, depth 1140-1320 ppm;
        S46 (.01):      T14 5.8-6.3 h, depth 1385-1445 ppm;
        S72 (.03):      T14 7.5-8.4 h, depth  900-1040 ppm.
    - TOI-5696.03 has no K2 event of its own. K2 alone excludes 62-64% of log P (5-1500 d),
      including essentially all P < 30 d.

  TOI-5523.01 (28,200 ppm, 13.6 h, M dwarf; K2-43): no K2 event.
    - 5 candidates were flagged, each in only one reduction (4 SAP, 1 EVEREST). Each is excluded
      or near zero in PDC and K2SFF. The star varies by about 2% on day timescales
      (plots/toi5523.01_*.png).
    - K2 C1 is flat. It excludes P < ~39 d, and 63% of log P overall in both PDC and K2SFF.
    - Separately: K2-43 has 2 TESS sectors (S63, S72) observed after this TOI's 2022 update.

  Bottom line: zero new K2 hits.
  Duplication check (ExoFOP JSON, pscomppars, arXiv):
    - TOI-5560.01: our own k2singles result (RNAAS submitted).
    - TOI-5696: the K2 events are already in the ExoFOP TOI ephemerides; .02 is validated
      (Collier+2026).
    - TOI-5523.01: no hit, so nothing to check.

4. NEW TESS DATA  (scripts/s04_new_tess.py -> new_tess.csv; s05_exofop_check.py, s08_summary.py)
  Sector timing: tess-point sectors, counted as observed if the sector ended before 2026-10-12.
  - Sectors observed after "Date TOI Updated":
      100/156 TOIs have at least one (464 sector-observations; median 3 among those).
      56 have none.
  - Sectors observed beyond the TOI's own "Sectors" list: 116 TOIs (615 sector-observations).
  - Stale "single as of sector N" comments (later sectors observed): 24 of the 35 that name a sector.
  - ExoFOP per-target JSON (156/156 fetched): a post-update community period exists for 15 TOIs,
    plus TOI-7044.01 resolved in its comment (16 in total):
      - by user: slee 5, mason 5, lafargamagro 2 (RAVEN), osborn, vincent, lhill;
      - nearly all dated 2025-26 and using S86-S105;
      - examples: 772.02 -> 32.36 d; 1894.01 -> 369.60 d; 4629.01 -> 51.05 d; 5990.01 -> 38.94 d;
        2300.03 -> 1252.78 or 626.39 d; 4187.02 -> 283.11 or 141.55 d.
  - Remaining open pool with new data and no newer period: 88 TOIs (416 new sector-observations).
      52 are true no-period singles; 20 have a max period.
      47 have >= 3 new sectors; 48 have Tmag < 10; 33 have >= 3 new sectors and Tmag < 11.
      10 are on confirmed-planet hosts.
  - Not searched (as instructed). Caveats:
      - "Not updated" does not mean "not searched": SPOC multi-sector runs that find nothing do not
        update the TOI row.
      - S105-S108 products may not all be public yet.
      - Uploads were matched on the exact "TOI x.xx" field, so uploads made under the old CTOI
        name before promotion could be missed.
