EPIC 202126877 (TYC 1883-1721-1) is a 21.775-d eccentric binary of two A stars whose eclipses have appeared within the last two decades. SuperWASP and KELT saw no eclipse in 2004-2008 (<4.5%, 3 sigma). K2 caught a 6.2% primary and no secondary in 2014. TESS measured a primary 20.6% deep in 2021, 27.4% in 2023 and 35.6% in 2026, and a secondary growing from 4.4% to 12.5%. Two independent eclipse models give an orbital inclination rising from 85.7 deg (2014) to 88.5-88.8 deg (2026). The rate rose from 0.20 deg/yr (2014-2021) to 0.31-0.36 deg/yr (2021-2026). The primary eclipses probably began in 2007-2011 and could become total, about 40-45% deep, in 2027-2031. The interval between primary and secondary eclipses changes by up to 26 min from one orbit to the next, so e cos(omega) varies over months. Both effects point to a close third body.
