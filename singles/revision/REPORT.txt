Bottom line for the editor: in no case did a pipeline light curve (SAP or PDCSAP) show the
catalogued event where our pipeline product missed it because of photometry. The causes are the
CTOI entries themselves (epoch errors 9, wrong TIC 3, catalogued depth overstated 5, no event 6,
no data 1), the event-test thresholds (8 below SNR 5), quality flags (1) and a blend (1). The one
product-dependent case inside (c) is TIC 38005437, where PDCSAP suppresses a dip that SAP,
TESS-SPOC and our photometry all show; and in TIC 152283270 the only feature is 2-min-aperture-only.
Counting (b)+(c)+(d) and 354032500, the event is present in TESS data for 24 of the 34.

PART 2 (Q4). VALIDATION OF OUR TESSCUT APERTURE PHOTOMETRY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method under test (singles/scripts/tesscut_photometry.py): 15x15-px TESScut cutout; aperture = pixels
within 1.6 px of the WCS position AND above the cutout's 60th-percentile median flux (8-9 px; 4 px
for the T=15.5 star); background = per-frame median of the faintest 40% of pixels; quality == 0.
No crowding or flux-fraction correction. Reference: SPOC 2-min SAP_FLUX/PDCSAP_FLUX of the same
sector, quality == 0, averaged over each FFI exposure (>= 60% coverage).

Sample (11 stars, T = 8.5-15.5): TIC 147035057 S33 (original case, 600-s FFIs); eight CP/KP TOIs in
S91-S107 (200-s FFIs; S103/105/106/107 are the regime in which we used own photometry):
TOI-267 S105, TOI-105 S106, TOI-102 S98, TOI-192 S103, TOI-774 S91, TOI-7716 S107, TOI-108 S95,
TOI-7501 S92; non-transiting TIC 65290698 S105; faint TIC 1461133 S93 (T=15.5, CROWDSAP 0.04, out of
range, reported for completeness).

(i) Absolute flux. For T < 11.5 our flux is 0.78-0.99 of SAP_FLUX and 0.74-0.83 of PDCSAP_FLUX
    (0.80-0.87 of the zero-point expectation, against 1.05-1.09 for PDCSAP): the flux lost outside
    our 8-9-pixel aperture, which is smaller than SPOC's (13-26 px) for bright stars; the ratio is
    stable within a sector (16-84%: 0.7975-0.7980 for TIC 147035057). For T = 12.4-14.1, where SPOC
    uses 4-7 px, our 8-px aperture collects 1.14-1.59x SAP and 0.89-1.13x PDCSAP, i.e. more
    contaminating flux (CROWDSAP 0.71-0.96). Background per pixel agrees with SAP_BKG/N_ap to
    1-9% (29% for the T=15.5 star), correlation >= 0.97.
(ii) Normalised light curves. Point-to-point scatter per FFI cadence: own/SAP median 1.07
    (0.90-1.75); 1-h scatter after a 1-d running median: own/SAP median 1.13 (0.89-5.0).
    Equal or better than SPOC for T >= 11.3; worse for the brightest stars (TOI-267 T=8.9:
    1.75x p2p, 5x at 1 h; TOI-105: 1.3x, 1.7x), where the small aperture makes the flux pointing-
    sensitive. Cadence retention: own 80-100% vs SPOC 58-94% (SPOC flags more scattered light).
(iii) Transits. Depths own/PDCSAP = 0.97-1.02 for the six uncrowded systems (T = 8.5-11.4,
    CROWDSAP >= 0.94), own/SAP 1.00-1.03. For the three crowded faint hosts own/PDCSAP = 0.86, 0.74,
    0.69 (own/SAP 0.89, 0.97, 0.82): we apply no crowding correction, so depths there are diluted.
    The original case TIC 147035057 S33: 3631 vs 3740 ppm PDCSAP (0.97) and 3542 SAP; the README's
    3.21 vs 3.28 ppt used box depths and is consistent. Mid-times: mean per-transit own-PDCSAP
    offsets -2.0 to +1.7 min (|offset| <= 0.8 min for 7 of 9), rms 0.1-3.0 min (7.7 min at T=14.1),
    in line with the own-photometry timing errors (median 0.4-7 min).
(iv) Systematics. Within a data segment our curve follows SAP (not PDCSAP: we do no cotrending):
    after per-segment normalisation the 1-h binned own vs SAP correlation is 0.76-1.00 for 8 of 10
    (0.18 and -0.21 for TOI-102 and TOI-267, two of the three brightest 200-s cases). The main difference is LEVEL STEPS of the
    own/SAP ratio between data segments: median 13 ppt (0.8-59 ppt) across gaps > 1 h, median 5 ppt
    across gaps > 0.5 d. The event and repeat tests fit a free offset per segment only at gaps > 0.5 d
    (alias_pipeline.SEG_GAP), so a step at a momentum dump (short gap) inside a test window is NOT
    absorbed except by the local polynomial.

CAVEAT worth a look before the revision (not acted on): the three tentative S107 repeats rest
only on own photometry (268534931, 139726266, 168702709), and 268534931's egress gap coincides
with a momentum dump across which "the flux level jumps by ~5 ppt" (singles/README.md). That is
the step behaviour found here. Wording in the note should keep them tentative.

  "We validated this photometry against SPOC 2-min light curves of 11 stars (T = 8.5-15.5)
  in Sectors 33 and 91-107, including eight known transiting planets. Our 8-9-pixel aperture
  collects 0.78-0.99 of the SPOC SAP flux for T < 11.5 (more than SAP for fainter, crowded stars,
