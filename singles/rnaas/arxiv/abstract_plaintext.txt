Community TESS Objects of Interest (CTOIs) flagged as single transits are rarely revisited. I re-examined 303 such stars against all TESS data through Sector 108, with a validated repeat search, difference imaging and Gaia DR3 binarity diagnostics. I recover the catalogued event for 272 stars; the other 31 mostly have a wrong epoch, a weaker dip than catalogued, or no dip. Of the 272, 79 show repeats: 21 are Gaia-flagged binaries, 18 are blends, 23 fit no single period, and 17 needed vetting by hand. An any-depth search shows that the CTOI 147035057.01 event is the secondary eclipse of a 37.315 d eccentric binary with a 5.5% primary. Two on-target candidates with two transits survive: TIC 159614322, where a partial third transit leaves P = 89.965 or 179.930 d, and TIC 229055790. For 193 candidates that remain single, a median 52% of log-period space (5-1500 d) is now excluded.
