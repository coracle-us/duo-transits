## 2. Inconclusive epochs, per-epoch tables (4ac26d3), plus a bug fix (2c5249f)
- Epochs with coverage under 0.8, or within 0.5 d of a gap or sector edge, are

- **Bug found and fixed (2c5249f):** coverage used one median cadence for the
  whole mixed light curve. Every 30-min sector (S1-S26) therefore scored about
  0.33 and was ignored by the alias step, in round 1 too.

Verdicts:

| Star, P | Excluding epochs, old rule -> now | Survives? |
|---|---|---|
| 230133616, 23.7535 d | 5 -> 5 (S49, S73, S79, S83 x2) | No |
| 275647095, 17.064 d | 5 -> 4 (S37 edge epoch now inconclusive; S64, S90 x2, S100 still exclude) | No |
| 241550976, 21.197 d | 3 -> 1 (S101, S102 inconclusive; S64 still excludes) | No |
| 40001645, 11.99 d | none | Supported |
| 274858362, 10.59 d | none | Supported |

The real cause is different from what the brief suggested:
- Every epoch that still excludes is clean and **holds a transit**, at 0.61-0.88x
  the reference box depth.
- The rule max(depth0 - 3 sigma, 0.5 depth0) excludes any epoch more than
  3 sigma shallower than the reference.
- For 275647095 and 241550976 the reference is TESS-SPOC and the excluding
  epochs are QLP.
- On the CNN's calibrated errors most of those epochs sit within about 2 sigma
  of the paper depth (230133616 S49: 5534 +/- 349 vs 6110 ppm). Only S73
  (230133616) and S100 (275647095) are about 3.5 sigma shallower.

**Decision for the channel:** should an epoch exclude only when it is also
below 0.5 depth0? Under that rule all three periods survive. Not changed here.

## 5. TIC 230133616 pixel check (cc054de)
- **The transits are in the old data.** Every covered predicted epoch has a dip:
  11/11 in S47-S60 (4150-5560 ppm; 5 more fall in gaps) and 10 in S16-S26
  (4730-6470 ppm). With the paper event and S73-S86 that is 34 epochs with a
  measured dip at 23.75 d. Not a single-transit candidate.

- TESScut difference images for S40, S49, S53, S56, S81, S82 and S84 all peak
  within 0.6 px of the target. Difference-image centroids are 1.9-3.9" from the target
  (13" in S40). In- vs out-of-transit centroid shifts are 2.2 mpix or less.
  Aperture depths are 4720-5970 ppm, including S49, which the alias step marks
  "excludes".
- Gaia DR3: 10 sources within 2' brighter than T+6.
  - The target has **RUWE 3.79**, so it is possibly an unresolved binary.
  - A G = 12.5 star 90" away shows no dimming.
- The signal is on target to within the TESS pixel scale. A bound companion
  can't be ruled out.

## 6. QLP S94+ timing (d20841f)
- None of these sectors has TESS-SPOC or SPOC, so the reference is our TESScut
  photometry on a corrected clock.
- Method:
  - The target's Roemer delay comes from the JPL Horizons TESS ephemeris. It
    matches TESS-SPOC TIMECORR to 0.001 s.
  - TESScut's own TIMECORR is for another direction (-26 to +50 s off at these
    events). That has been removed.

| QLP file | QLP minus true BJD |
|---|---|
| S65 control, S95 (302377820), S99 (152464302) | +1.9 s, constant: the file follows the target's correction |
| S100 241550976 | -41 s |
| S100 275647095 | -106 s |
| S101 107113048 | -181 s |
| S101 438588300 | -229 s |
| S101 275647095 | -233 s and -184 s |
| S101 241550976 | -307 s |
| S102 241550976 | -363 s (only modulo 200 s; row counts differ) |

- S100-S102 apply one constant correction per sector. In this sample it starts
  at S100, not S94, and it is larger than "up to 2 min": up to -385 s within
  those sectors.
- Trapezoid refits on QLP and on TESScut agree within their 4-12 min errors;
  they are too coarse to see this.
- results/round2/qlp_s94_timing.csv gives tc_tesscut (the non-QLP time to use)
  and tc_qlp_clock_corrected.

## Addendum, 2026-11-01: epoch exclusion rule switched
Jon decided the section 2 question. An epoch now excludes a period only when its box depth is below
0.5 depth0 - 3 sigma. Clean epochs between that and depth0 - 3 sigma are marked `shallow` and exclude
nothing. Test: EPOCH_RULE_TEST_2026-11-01.md.

- Why: on 83 confirmed planets the old rule excluded the true period for 68, this rule for 4. A box mean
  is typically 0.73-0.93 of a fitted depth, and the old rule read that offset as no transit.
- Reran: aliases, report, and the section 4 and 5 scripts. Search, common-period flags and CNN don't
  use this rule. The 53 shortlist candidates and their order are unchanged.
- Allowed or supported aliases, 53 events: median 17 -> 21, mean 19.4 -> 27.0; 23 events unchanged.
- Quoted periods, 39 in all: excluded 27 -> 19, supported 12 -> 19, allowed 0 -> 1.
  - Excluded -> supported: 230133616 at 23.753 d, 241550976 at 21.197 d, 275647095 at 17.064 d, and
    404736082b at 8.921 d.
  - Excluded -> allowed: 317132251 at 25.210 d.

  275647095 S100 remain the epochs to check for a real depth change.
