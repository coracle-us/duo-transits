## How it was checked

- **Files AAS holds.** The SHA-256 of every file in the portal was compared with the local file. All source files
  (TeX, BIB/BBL, figures, cls/bst, ORCID logo) are byte-identical to the audited sources. The AAS84033 and
  AAS84041 merged PDFs are byte-identical to our tectonic builds (`McBee_AAS84033_R1.pdf`, 107,735 B;
  `McBee_AAS84041_R1.pdf`, 236,450 B). The other three merged PDFs were built by AAS from those identical files. They
  were viewed in the portal: AAS84038 3 pages, AAS84040 3 pages, AAS84043 4 pages, the same layout as our local
  builds. AAS's own pdfTeX build of the new K2 TeX gives exactly the same pdftotext counts as our build.
- **Word counts.** Two independent counts:
  - "gate": `scripts/rnaas_gate.py` rules (branch `rnaas-word-gate`), main text = everything from `\end{abstract}`
    to `\end{document}`, references and table included;
  - "pdf": pdftotext of the built PDF, everything after the keywords.

  Calibration against the two AAS quality-check mails, using the bounced versions rebuilt from their tags:

  | Bounced version | AAS abstract | AAS main | gate | pdf | AAS/gate | AAS/pdf |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | AAS84033 (tag `aas84033-submitted`) | 138 | 1,640 | 1,605 | 1,566 | 1.022 | 1.047 |
  | AAS84041 (tag `aas84041-submitted`) | 143 | 1,650 ("over by 300") | 1,662 | 1,650 | 0.993 | 1.000 |

  AAS's estimate below is gate x 0.993–1.022. The K2 pdf ratio is higher because pdftotext splits the table
  differently. AAS's abstract counts match the plain-text abstract in the form (K2 form 139, AAS 138; EB form 143,
  AAS 143), so the form abstract is what AAS counts.
- **Guidelines** (journals.aas.org/research-note-preparation-guidelines, read 2026-11-25):
  - The total limit is 1,500 words, "includes title, headers, captions, and references with 150 words reserved
    for the required abstract".
  - At most one figure or table.
  - "table contents do not count against the word count of a Note, caption text will".
  - A figure with its caption "must fit on a single print page".
  - Long tables "should only show the first 5 – 10 data lines".
  - The counting method AAS names is `texcount -v3 -merge -incbib -dir -sub=none -utf8 -sum`, and "the AAS
    Editorial office's word count is the final arbiter". The AAS84033 count (1,640, above our gate count, which
    includes the table body) suggests AAS's tool counted the deluxetable body anyway. The gate keeps counting it.
- **References.** Every `\bibitem` (or `.bbl` entry) was looked up in ADS through the API: by DOI, by arXiv/ASCL
  id, or by first author, year, journal, volume and page. Cited keys were compared with listed keys in both
  directions.
- **Dataset DOIs** were resolved through doi.org and their titles read from DataCite. **UAT keywords** were checked
  against `astrothesaurus/UAT` `UAT_list.json` (v6.0.0). **Repos and Zenodo:** `coracle-us/duo-transits` and
  `coracle-us/gaia-dr4` are public (HTTP 200 without login); doi:10.5281/zenodo.24506629 resolves.

## Gaps in our own tooling found by this audit

1. `scripts/rnaas_gate.py` does not expand `\bibliography{}`, so a note using a `.bib` file passes with its
   references uncounted (AAS84038: 1,028 instead of ≈1,292).
2. The gate does not catch body text placed before the first `\section`, which AASTeX 7 typesets above the title
   (AAS84041).
3. No page-1 render check before upload. The broken AAS84041 page went out because the PDF was not looked at
   after the length edit.
4. `texcount`, AAS's own counter, is not installed here (no TeX Live). Installing it would replace the
   0.993–1.022 calibration with AAS's method. It needs a download, so it waits for Jon's OK.
